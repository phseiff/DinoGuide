DinoGuide

Imagine you were a time traveler about to travel 66 million years back in time, back to when dinosaurs ruled the earth. Which dinosaurs would you actually meet at your location? What if you traveled 160 million years back in time instead? Or 215 million? What about other regions of the world? And which of the dinosaurs you might meet had meat, and therefore you, on the menu?

This educational website attempts to answer all of these questions.

(DinoGuide is currently in its alpha phase and therefore only lists european dinosaurs.)
